## Setup failures and fixes

The plain `4b` alias selected MLX/nvfp4 and returned HTTP 501 for structured output. The explicit GGUF tag passed; the unused MLX download was removed. Free-text model quotes edited whitespace/markup or inserted ellipses and were rejected. Generation now chooses original source-ID/excerpt pairs; the final provenance validator is unchanged. Non-thinking constrained chat falsely abstained; all-thinking extraction exhausted its token cap. Controlled comparisons selected fast extraction and thinking chat. Manual Resume also now clears an obsolete rate delay after successful GitHub verification, while automatic retries still honor reset times.

## Limits and delivery

This is a small real-repo acceptance sample, not proof of general model accuracy or paraphrase entailment. Bounded fragments may omit context; character budgets differ from token budgets. Huge repos take API/model time, inaccessible endpoints remain partial, and the local deployment supports one worker per SQLite DB. Broader PEP/forum ingestion, authentication, semantic reranking and a complete changed-path graph remain out of scope. Original screenshot/PDF and auxiliary pack documents were missing.

Changes are in [PR #1](https://github.com/millimeow/DevRecall/pull/1) on `feat/devrecall-v3`, with no merge to main. Baseline PR/branch CI passed; follow-up CI is checked on its actual pushed revision separately from local checks. HACKATHON-only roots `c4833b4` and `6c9d3b9` are preserved. Inherited root `b744502` contains LICENSE/README and remains a disclosed first-commit compliance gap; the CI audit warns rather than rewriting history.
